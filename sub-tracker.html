Help

Teams

Each team has its own roster, game format, timer duration, colours, and sub-suggestion settings. Create teams and switch between them from the "Your Teams" list — tap Select to load one, or Edit to change its settings.

Player list & presence

Build a full squad of any size in team options — add, rename, remove, and reorder players there. Each player has a Present/Absent toggle; only present players are included when you start a game. Up to 12 players can be present at once.

Starting a game

The setup screen shows your present players and count (e.g. 8/12). Reorder with the ▲▼ arrows, or toggle presence right there. The top 5 present players are outlined — they'll start on court.

During the game

The clock counts down each quarter/half. Tap Next Period to advance and reset the clock (it'll confirm first if time's still on the clock). Once the final period hits 0:00, a New Game button appears in its place — player times keep counting past 0:00 in case of overtime.

Substitutions

Tap any player's bar to sub them on or off court — on-court players get a thick outline. Sub suggestions highlight who to bring off (orange) and who to bring on (teal), in one of two modes set per team:

Court Time — suggests the players with the most time on court, and the least time on the bench.
Next On List — ignores time and just follows your fixed roster order, rotating players to the bottom of the list once subbed.

The number of suggestions is configurable per team and automatically scales down if your bench is small. Suggestions freeze mid-swap (only shrinking, never adding new names) until the court is back to a full 5.

Fouls

Use "Player Foul Limit Reached" below the roster to mark a player fouled out. They're automatically taken off court, moved to the bottom of the list with a "Fouled Off" tag, and excluded from all sub suggestions for the rest of the game.

Sub reminder cues

Set per team in options: once the "time since last sub" clock passes a set length (default 3 min), the app plays a sound and/or vibrates as a reminder, repeating at a set interval (default every 30 sec) until the next sub happens. Sound and vibration can each be toggled independently, or cues turned off entirely. Vibration isn't supported on iPhone (a platform limitation, not this app).

Colours & appearance

Set a background colour, player bar colour, logo, and on-court highlight colour per team in options. Text and panel colours automatically adjust for contrast against whatever background you pick.

Data & screen

Everything is saved on this device/browser only. Use App Settings (🛠️) to back up, restore, or clear your data. The screen is kept awake automatically while a game is running.
